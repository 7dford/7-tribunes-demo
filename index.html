Editorial Method

Neutral brief first. Perspective second.

Every short take is built from the multi-source factual spine, not one publisher's wording. Loaded phrasing is removed while confirmed facts, material disputes and uncertainty are preserved. 7 Tribunes separates reliability from ideology. Entertainment, celebrity and gossip items are treated as cross-cutting content and may appear inside any relevant Pillar when genuinely newsworthy and when they meet verification, privacy and decency guardrails. A source can lean left or right and still score highly for reporting quality. The platform first identifies what is confirmed, reported, disputed, unknown, or corrected—then compares how credible reporting frames the same story.

7 Tribunes Method

Balance without false equivalence

1. Establish the fact spine. Identify confirmed, reported, disputed, unknown and changed information using multiple independent sources.

2. Score reporting quality separately. Reliability considers sourcing, evidence, headline accuracy, transparency, corrections and reporting-versus-opinion separation.

3. Analyze perspective. Framing analysis considers language, emphasis, quoted authorities, contextual differences and ideological positioning. Ratings include confidence.

4. Select credible comparisons. The app never chooses a low-quality article merely to create left/right symmetry.

5. Explain the result. Users can see why a story or article was selected and where uncertainty remains.
